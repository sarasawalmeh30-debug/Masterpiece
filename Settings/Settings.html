TEAMLY POLICY

Terms & Conditions

Draft — requires review; not published

These draft Terms & Conditions describe proposed rules for accessing and using the TEAMLY service marketplace. They require review and approval before they can be treated as official terms.

1. About TEAMLY

TEAMLY is designed to help clients discover service providers, including individuals and companies, and to support service requests and project coordination. Features shown in this prototype may not be connected to live services.

2. User Accounts

Users should provide accurate registration information and keep their sign-in details private. The current prototype uses browser-based demo state and does not provide server-side account authentication or password management.

3. Client Responsibilities

Clients are responsible for providing accurate project details, reviewing provider information, and agreeing directly with a provider on the scope, schedule, price, and any cancellation conditions before proceeding.

4. Provider Responsibilities

Individual providers and companies should provide accurate service, portfolio, pricing, and availability information, communicate clearly, and only accept work they reasonably expect to fulfill.

5. Service Requests and Agreements

Submitting a request does not guarantee that a provider will accept it. The parties should confirm the scope, timing, price, and other conditions of their arrangement with each other.

6. Payments and Fees

Payment screens or records in this prototype do not establish that live payment processing is available. No platform commission, refund, deposit, cancellation, or payment policy is stated by this draft; any such terms must be confirmed and published before use.

7. Prohibited Conduct

Users should not misuse the service, impersonate others, engage in fraud or harassment, attempt unauthorized access, or use the platform for unlawful purposes.

8. Account Restrictions

Any account restriction or termination process must be defined and implemented before this section is relied upon as an enforceable policy.

9. Changes to These Terms

When approved terms are changed, TEAMLY should identify the effective date and communicate material changes through an appropriate channel.

10. Contact

For assistance, use the existing Help & Support page. This draft does not add a separate contact address.

TEAMLY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Draft — requires review; not published

This draft describes data handling visible in the current TEAMLY prototype. It is not a complete or legally approved privacy notice. The production data flows, operators, retention periods, and user request process need to be confirmed before publication.

1. Information Used in This Prototype

Registration and provider profile screens can collect information such as a name, email address, account type, service details, location, pricing, profile description, and portfolio material. The exact information depends on the screen and information a user chooses to enter.

2. Where Prototype Data Is Stored

Some profile and demo records are saved in browser local storage, while a demo sign-in flag is kept in session storage. This is browser-side prototype storage, not a verified server account system. Data saved in a browser may remain on that device until cleared and may be accessible to someone with access to the browser profile.

3. How Information Is Used

Within the prototype, entered information supports profile displays, service discovery, requests, messaging, and demo administrative views where those screens are implemented. This description does not verify a live service operation or a production data processing purpose.

4. Information Visibility

Provider profile details and portfolio material may be displayed to users through profile and discovery screens. Project or request details may appear in the relevant prototype screens. Exact access controls and sharing behavior must be verified before launch.

5. Passwords and Security

This prototype does not provide a backend password update or verified server-side authentication flow. Do not enter a password you use elsewhere. This page does not claim particular security safeguards or that information is completely secure.

6. Payments and Messages

Payment-related and messaging screens are present in the project, but this review did not establish live payment processing or a complete production data flow. Do not treat this draft as a statement about a payment processor or third-party sharing.

7. Retention and Requests

No production retention period or implemented account deletion process was verified. Users who need assistance can use the existing Help & Support page; this prototype does not promise a particular deletion outcome or response time.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The reviewed prototype uses browser local storage and session storage for certain demo state. No analytics service or cookie policy was verified in this review.

9. Policy Updates

Before publication, approved policy text should include its effective date and explain how material changes will be communicated.

10. Contact

For assistance, use the existing Help & Support page. A dedicated privacy contact and request process have not been verified.
