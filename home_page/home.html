Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about how TEAMLY works.

What is TEAMLY?

TEAMLY is a platform that helps clients discover suitable service providers and build teams for their projects.

How does TEAMLY work?

Describe your project, review service recommendations, choose providers, and manage your project through the platform.

Can I choose more than one provider?

Yes. You can select multiple providers for different services within the same project.

Do I have to use the AI assistant?

No. You can start by choosing the service you are looking for, or use the AI assistant if you need help deciding.

Can service providers join TEAMLY?

Service providers can create an account and present their services to potential clients.
